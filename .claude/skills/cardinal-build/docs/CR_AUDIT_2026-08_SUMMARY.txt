================================================================
THE 8 SERIOUS ONES
================================================================

1. CONVERT-TO-CONTRACT IS A DEAD BUTTON
   It calls an address that doesn't exist. The file behind it was
   renamed at some point (a dash where there used to be an
   underscore) and the button never followed. The first time
   anyone converts an AI estimate, it fails. Nobody has hit it yet
   only because no AI estimates exist.
   Fix: one word. Minutes.

2. "SEND" TELLS YOUR REP THE CLIENT GOT AN EMAIL. NOBODY DID.
   The AI-estimate Send button marks it sent and pops "client will
   receive email/SMS." I checked every file in the app: nothing
   sends an email or a text when an estimate is marked sent, and
   nothing ever has. It also ignores errors, so even a refused
   save still shows the success message.
   Fix: your call — wire a real sender, or change the words.

3. JOB MONEY IS COMPUTED TWO DIFFERENT WAYS
   Your client profile counts ONLY signed contracts. Every list,
   report, and the receivables card counts the best estimate. So a
   job with a signed estimate and no contract reads $20,525 on one
   screen and $0 on another. This is exactly what you ran into on
   the Joeseph job — it isn't one job, it's 4 of your 30 right now.
   Fix: one story for job value, or a label on each screen saying
   where the number came from. Product call, not a bug fix.

4. THE RECEIVABLES CARD NEVER SUBTRACTS PAYMENTS
   A half-paid job shows its FULL value as outstanding on the home
   dashboard, while the job's own page correctly shows the balance.
   The card also says its totals come from the worksheet; they
   don't — they come from estimates.
   Fix: subtract what's been paid, fix the caption. Small.

5. SUPPLEMENTS HAVE TWO SEPARATE TRACKERS THAT CAN'T SEE EACH OTHER
   File a supplement on the client profile card and the claims
   screen shows nothing. File one on the claims screen and the
   pipeline, the "owed by carriers" figure, and the chase list
   never move. They don't even use the same words for status —
   one says "filed," the other says "submitted."
   Nothing is broken TODAY only because no supplements are filed
   yet. The first real one splits your numbers.
   Fix: one tracker. Your three-filings model (partial denial /
   backend / paid-when-incurred + COC) is already written down in
   the build log — I'd bring you a shape before building.

6. YOUR ONE CLAIM WITH REAL MONEY IS INVISIBLE
   $28,727.17 approved. It's in the database, correctly linked to
   its job — and it appears on NO insurance screen. Two reasons
   stacked: the job's record is missing the marker that says
   "insurance," and its stage (On Hold) is one the insurance
   pipeline silently drops on the floor. Fixing the first still
   leaves it hidden by the second.
   Fix: repair that record + three small code fixes. Small.

7. TWO DEVICES CAN ERASE EACH OTHER'S WORK
   There's no live sync. When anything saves, it writes the job's
   whole record back from what that device had in memory. So if
   you're on the phone and Joan is on the desktop on the same job,
   whoever saves second wipes the other's changes — the whole
   message thread, payments, tasks, contacts, stage timestamps.
   You work from a phone AND a desktop, so this is real, not
   theoretical.
   Fix: cheapest version is re-read-before-save. Small, and it
   removes most of the risk.

8. PHOTO ACTIVITY DOWNLOADS ABOUT 12 MB EVERY TIME YOU TAP IT
   13 old photos are stored as raw data inside the database row
   instead of as files (11.8 MB of them). Opening Photo Activity
   pulls all of it before a single picture appears. Separately, 3
   cover photos add roughly 1 MB to every single app start and
   every save-refresh. And the photo gallery loads full-size
   originals into thumbnail-sized tiles.
   Fix: move those 16 images to file storage, plus a one-line
   change on the gallery. Biggest speed win in the whole list,
   smallest risk.

================================================================
INSURANCE: WHAT THE SPEC ASKED FOR THAT DOESN'T EXIST
================================================================

Each of these needs your shape before I build anything.

* Adjuster MEETINGS — appointments have no adjuster type, no
  calendar sync, no reminder. (Adjuster contacts and the directory
  DO exist.)
* Scope history — only the first scope and the latest survive.
  Scope 2 of 4 is gone forever, and no line-item detail is kept
  anywhere. The AI reads the totals off a scope, not the lines.
* Supplement unification — see item 5 above. This one is the
  foundation; the three below get easier after it.
* PWI as an expense ledger — right now "PWI" only exists as a
  label on a check that came IN. Nothing tracks a PWI line item
  through unincurred → incurred → billed → paid.
* Certificate of completion + depreciation release — the dollar
  figure exists and gets chased, but there's no release-requested
  or release-received state, and no COC document type.
* A carrier-facing final invoice — today's invoice is retail-
  shaped: it needs a signed contract and knows nothing about a
  claim, RCV, or depreciation.

ONE WARNING ON THE SPEC: it proposed new pipeline stages
(Adjuster Scheduled, Scope Pending, Depreciation Released...).
Do NOT add those as stages. Any stage the app doesn't recognize
silently turns that job into a Lead. Insurance sub-status belongs
in the claim record, where it already lives.
