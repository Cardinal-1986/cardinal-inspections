# Cardinal app — end-to-end button and page audit

**14 Oct 2026 · build 1340 · phone (390 px) and desktop (1440 px) · signed in as admin · test data only**

## Verdict

I tapped 806 buttons, menu entries and tiles across 31 screens at both sizes. I found no unwired buttons: every candidate I re-tested turned out to do something. Back got you off every screen. No tap caused a script error. No page scrolled sideways. Five things are really wrong:

- One menu item always opens a blank page.
- One Close button leaves you on a blank page.
- The phone Tools menu runs off the screen.
- The floating "Ask" button covers Save on the client form on a phone.
- Pop-up forms stay open when you swipe Back on a phone.

None of them traps you. A tap on Menu or the top bar always gets you out.

- **Self Check (admin):** Back closes it but drops you on the Production Board, not where you started. Esc does nothing.
- **Phone Menu:** Back and Esc don't close the menu. Tapping outside it or picking an item does.
- **Switch portal screen:** there is no Cancel or ×. You leave by picking a portal (your current one is ticked) or by swiping Back.
- **Small tap targets** (under the 44 px finger size):
  - the estimate builder's side sections on desktop (about 30 px tall)
  - the Crews tabs (26 px) and crew list rows (36 px)
  - the stage arrow on a client (40 px wide)
  - the document editor's Save button on desktop (42 px)

## Technical notes (for the engineer)

Line numbers are for `index.html` at build 1340.

**1. Financing Rates — `display:none` on a class-shown view.**
- `hideAllViews()` runs `_fin.style.display='none'` at **19905–19906**.
- `#cr-fin` is shown by a class: `#cr-fin.open{display:flex}` at **92724–92725**.
- `open()` at **92898–92903** calls `ensure()`, then `hideAllViews()`, then `classList.add('open')`. The inline style always wins.
- Measured: class `open`, inline `display:none`, computed `none`, `body.style.overflow='hidden'`.
- This is the "close lever must match how it is shown" rule.
- Fix: use `classList.remove('open')` in `hideAllViews`.

**2. Suppliers ×.**
- `open()` at **67579–67592** calls `hideAllViews()`, which hides `#mainView`.
- `close()` at **67594** only sets `el.style.display='none'` and never calls `showHome()`.
- Financing Rates' own `close()` (92900) already shows the pattern to copy.
- The ABC order sheet's `close()` (67778) is fine: its `open()` never hides the home.

**3. Tools dropdown.**
- `#crBanner .cbmenu{position:absolute;top:100%;left:0;min-width:215px…}` at **68684**.
- Markup is at 4186–4205.
- Measured at 390 px: Tools menu x 299–512, Production menu x 196–409.
- Fix: for the right-hand dropdown use `right:0;left:auto` and/or `max-width:calc(100vw - 16px)`.

**4. Ask button over modals.**
- `#cr-ai-fab` has `z-index:9400` (**90131**, markup **90171**); `#projModal` has `z-index:200` (**8294**).
- At full scroll on 390 × 844: `elementFromPoint` on `#pfSave`'s top edge returns `#cr-ai-fab` (Save at y 741–786, FAB at y 712–760).
- The same overlap was measured over:
  - `#puList` rows
  - the Cardinal Truth stage rail (`.cr-cth-stage`)
  - `#pfCategory`
  - on desktop, `#puList .pl-more`

**5. Back with a modal open.**
- `navRestore()` / `popstate` (29899–29990) restores views only; modals are never in history.
- Reproduced: profile p1 → Edit client info → `history.back()` gives `#projModal` still `display:block` over `#clientsView` (hash `#clients`). `#cdCrmBar` then paints over the form.
- The sweep saw the same for `#sigModal`, `#cr-est-picker`, `#cr-ped`, `#cr-ci`, `.cr-walk-bg`, `.cr-medmig-bg`, `.cr-cl-bg`, `.cr-ahc-bg` and the phone `#navMenu`.
- Escape is handled per module (18 sites); `projModal` and `sigModal` have none.

**Coverage gaps in this audit:**

- The inspection checklist modal: `openChecklist()` returns early without `currentProject`.
- Insurance, Production and Community portal-only menu sections: hidden for the Retail portal.
