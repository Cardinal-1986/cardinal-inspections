How to fill out this Cardinal inspection report

Everything highlighted in yellow is a fill-in field — tap it and type over it. This red panel only shows inside the Cardinal editor; it never prints, never exports to PDF, and never appears on a client share link.

  • Specs & findings: the Roofing Inspection Checklist fills the baseline specs and Areas of Concern rows automatically when the report is created.
  • AI drafting: tap a section’s re-analyze button to draft that section’s narrative from its photos.
  • Finish: Print / PDF makes the paper or PDF copy; Share link or Send delivers it to the client.
