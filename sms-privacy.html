SMS / Text Messaging Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 18, 2026

This policy explains how Cardinal Roofing and Renovations, LLC (“Cardinal,” “we,” “us”) handles information in connection with the text-message (SMS) notifications we send to our staff and subcontractors through our internal application at app.cardinalroster.com.

We do not share your mobile information for marketing. No mobile information — including your phone number and your consent to receive messages — will be shared with, sold to, rented to, or traded with third parties or affiliates for their marketing or promotional purposes. Text-messaging originator opt-in data and consent are never shared with any third party except as needed to deliver the messages (our messaging provider) or as required by law.

Who this applies to

Our text notifications are internal and operational. Recipients are Cardinal employees and subcontractors who provide their mobile number to Cardinal and agree to receive work-related notifications. We do not send marketing or promotional texts, and this program is not directed to consumers or the general public.

Information we collect

  • Your name and role;
  • Your mobile phone number; and
  • Your consent to receive operational text notifications, and any later opt-out.

How we use it

We use your mobile number solely to send operational notifications tied to your work — for example, a lead assigned to you, a task or reminder, and schedule alerts — and to let you opt out. We do not use it for advertising or marketing.

Message frequency, rates

Message frequency varies with job activity, typically about 1–10 messages per day per person. Message and data rates may apply, depending on your mobile carrier and plan.

Opting out and getting help

You can opt out of text messages at any time by replying STOP to any message. Reply HELP for help. After you reply STOP, we will stop sending operational texts to that number. You can opt back in by contacting us.

How we share information

We share your mobile number only with our messaging service provider for the sole purpose of delivering these messages, and only as required by law. As stated above, we never share it with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

Data security & retention

We keep mobile numbers and consent records only as long as needed for this program and our recordkeeping, and protect them using reasonable administrative and technical safeguards.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time; the “last updated” date above reflects the latest version.
